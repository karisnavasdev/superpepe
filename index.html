How to Buy Super Pepe (SUPEPE)

Note: Super Pepe (SUPEPE) is currently in its presale phase and is not available on Uniswap, PancakeSwap, or any other decentralized exchanges (DEXs). Please be cautious and avoid purchasing any fake SUPEPE tokens that may be circulating on DEXs, as they are scams.

How to Buy
Step 1: Prepare Your Wallet

Use a Wallet Connect supported wallet like MetaMask or Trust Wallet.

Step 2: Get ETH

Make sure you have ETH in your wallet for gas fees even if buying with USDT.

Step 3: Connect to the DApp
  1. Visit https://superpepe.io/
  2. Click “Connect Wallet”.
  3. Ensure Ethereum Chain (ERC20) is selected.
Step 4: Choose Payment Option

Select ETH or USDT as your payment method.

Step 5: Enter Purchase Amount
  1. Enter amount of ETH/USDT.
  2. See how many SUPEPE tokens you'll get.
  3. Click “Buy”.
Step 6: Verify and Confirm Transaction
  1. Confirm transaction in your wallet (e.g., MetaMask).
  2. Verify all details before approving.
  3. Wait for confirmation on Ethereum network.
Important: If paying with USDT, you must complete two transactions — one to approve the contract, and one to complete the purchase.
Step 7: Purchase Successful

Once confirmed, view your tokens on the Super Pepe Dashboard.
